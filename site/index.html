For AI agents

Your agents can go online from your boxes too.

Every membership includes an MCP server and an API. Give an agent a key and it can browse from home, pick a box per task, and keep an eye on usage, without a datacenter address that sites block.

  • list_boxesYour boxes, where they are and whether they're online.
  • fetchLoad a page through a chosen box and return it.
  • set_routeSend a device or an agent session through a box.
  • proxy_urlA SOCKS5 or HTTP proxy address for any tool that speaks proxy.
  • usageData used per box and per key, with your caps.

Questions
Good to know

    

      
Wire or Air?
Wire if the box sits next to your router: it's faster and steadier. Air anywhere else, as long as it reaches the Wi‑Fi.

      
Do I have to change my router settings?
No. Bento only makes outgoing connections, so there's nothing to open or configure.

      
Will it slow down my internet at home?
Your own traffic uses only what you send. Earn mode stays inside your cap and pauses when your household is busy.

      
Can my AI agent use it?
Yes. Add the Bento MCP server to any MCP client, or call the API or proxy with a key from the app. Agents use your boxes the same way your devices do, within the cap you set for each key.

      
How do I get paid?
Shared data is metered per kilobyte at $0.10 per GB. Earnings pay your membership first; the rest goes to your bank or PayPal each month.
