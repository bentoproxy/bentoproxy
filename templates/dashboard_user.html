Environment variables export ALL_PROXY="socks5h://api:{{ api_key }}@{{ socks5_host }}:{{ socks5_port }}" curl https://api.ipify.org # now exits through a residential node

Use socks5h://, not socks5:// — the h keeps DNS resolution at the exit node instead of on your machine.
